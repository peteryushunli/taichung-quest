# Starting location: Old Taichung Station plaza

## Current accepted baseline — 8 November 2026

Arrival is inside Old Station's welcome hall: language/welcome, the four-starter graphical selection, then a direct transition to the station plaza. Keep exactly Charmander, Bulbasaur, Squirtle and Pikachu as the starter choices; no alternate-start selector is planned. Existing/demo saves retain ordinary Continue without repeating the opening.

The separate shorter welcome/tutorial/settings revision remains a review proposal. PR8's map/gym consolidation preserves the accepted opening code; the older outdoor-start discussion below is historical where it conflicts with this baseline.

The user has accepted the recommended Old Taichung Station plaza starting point. The existing Greenway prototype spawn should migrate to the station district as the new world is built. Greenway and Maple Garden remain included landmarks; they are not alternate selectable starting locations.

## Accepted opening

Open with the player having just arrived in Taichung, standing outside the historic station. A fictional welcome-guide or research-guide character offers the four starters. The station and clock tower give the first screen a recognizable city identity.

Keep the sequence short: language choice, a few welcome lines, starter choice, map and immediate bike access, then free movement. A train cutscene, mandatory naming sequence, errands, and a guided tour are not necessary.

Miyahara and Taichung Park are natural nearby first destinations in the compressed old-city district. The map can also offer a westward connection toward the Greenway. These are game-route proposals, not real-world turn-by-turn directions or a fixed gym order.

This option creates a visitor-arrival story and starts the journey near an edge of the compressed city. Give the player multiple clear exits or destinations rather than forcing a linear tutorial route.

## Earlier alternative considered: Greenway / Civic Square

The strongest option for immediate free exploration from a central hub. It can support several short connections toward the museums, Shen Ji / Fine Arts, the old city, and the Theater / Maple Garden side.

It is already the prototype's playable foundation, which reduces implementation work, but existing code is not a reason to lock the final location. A fictional welcome kiosk can explain the starter selection without recreating the original professor tutorial.

## Earlier alternative considered: Maple Garden

Starts the player in a recognizable urban park on the Theater/Fengjia side of the working map. The lake and green space could establish local wildlife encounters early, and the city setting remains visible around the park.

The opening would emphasize discovering a Pokémon partner in a city park, rather than arriving by rail. It begins near the western edge of the current urban shortlist, so connect it clearly to the rest of the city.

Maple Garden reference: [official visitor information](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/attractions/intro/851). Its official tourism-dataset point is latitude `24.16801`, longitude `120.63935`, source ID `Attraction_387000000A_000818`.

## Rationale

- **Station** provides the accepted arrival-in-Taichung story.
- **Greenway** was the central-hub alternative.
- **Maple Garden** was the park-and-wildlife opening alternative.

In every case, preserve the requested brief intro, four native-ROM starter choices, immediate exploration, and accessible cycling. Starting location does not determine the player's final gym order.
