The user has accepted the retained landmark set, including the previously suggested additional places. The game decisions in `game-design.md` remain authoritative; detailed layouts, gym teams, and progression are open.

- **Excluded:** Gaomei Wetlands, Dajia Jenn Lann Temple, Luce Memorial Chapel, and Dakeng. The user has now confirmed removal of the latter two as well.

## Main urban shortlist

The selection balances recognizable city identity, distinct graphics at small scale, and different environments for exploration. Proposed game roles below are creative suggestions, not facts about the real places or finalized gym types.

The working set emphasizes the city center, Greenway, and Xitun. The ten places are included; detailed gym themes, teams, and implementation order remain open.

## Three accepted additional landmarks

- **Miyahara / 宮原眼科:** an old-city food/heritage landmark close to the station area.
- **Shen Ji New Village / 審計新村:** a creative-shop district near the Greenway cluster.
- **National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts / 國立臺灣美術館:** a cultural anchor in the Greenway/arts district.

These places are listed in the [official Taichung attractions catalog](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/attractions/listbyclassification); detailed visual briefs and factual visitor copy should be checked against their individual primary sources after selection.

## Compression implications to decide after the shortlist

Greenway, Civic Square, CMP, and nearby cultural stops can share a compact district instead of each consuming a separate large map. A district can still contain multiple landmark entrances if those landmarks are later selected as distinct gyms.

Do not reserve coastal or Dakeng districts based on the earlier recommendation. Work from the retained urban roster and use selected parks, gardens, waterways, or appropriate urban spaces for encounter planning.
