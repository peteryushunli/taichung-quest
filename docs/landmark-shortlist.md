# Proposed Taichung landmark shortlist

This is a researched recommendation for discussion, not a user-approved final roster. Do not expand implementation scope or replace the current visitor-guide catalog solely because a place is listed here. The game decisions in `game-design.md` remain authoritative; final gym count, teams, and map topology are open.

## Recommended eight anchors

The selection balances recognizable city identity, distinct graphics at small scale, and different environments for exploration. Proposed game roles below are creative suggestions, not facts about the real places or finalized gym types.

| Landmark | Identity to preserve | Proposed game contribution | Official reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old Taichung Station / 舊臺中車站 | Historic red-brick station and clock tower | Arrival point and an old-city landmark gym | [Taichung Station Railway Cultural Park](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/attractions/intro/1942) |
| Taichung Park / 臺中公園 | Lake and the recognizable Mid-Lake Pavilion | A historic park gym and a small pond/green-space habitat | [Taichung Tourism: classic attractions](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/Attractions/TopTen) |
| Calligraphy Greenway / 草悟道, with Civic Square | Green promenade and public square | First explorable district and urban garden habitat; chosen landmark can host its gym | [Calligraphy Greenway](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/Attractions/Intro/1050) |
| National Taichung Theater / 臺中國家歌劇院 | Distinctive curved architecture | A visually distinctive performance-themed gym | [Theater architecture](https://www.npac-ntt.org/en/about/architecture/design) |
| Fengjia Night Market / 逢甲夜市 | Dense food stalls, signs, and an evening atmosphere | Food/city-life district and a night-market gym; urban encounters are a later allocation choice | [Taichung Tourism: classic attractions](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/Attractions/TopTen) |
| Gaomei Wetlands / 高美濕地 | Wetland, boardwalk, wind turbines, and sunset | Coast/wetland environment and a fictional visitor-center or ranger gym | [Gaomei visitor center](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/attractions/intro/1653), [city landscape reference](https://www.taichung.gov.tw/media/433652/%E4%BE%86%E5%8E%BB%E6%8B%8D%E5%8F%B0%E4%B8%AD-%E7%AC%AC%E5%8D%81%E4%B8%80%E5%96%AE%E5%85%83%E8%87%B3%E9%99%84%E9%8C%84.pdf) |
| National Museum of Natural Science / 國立自然科學博物館 | Science and natural-history identity, with dinosaur/exhibit cues | Research-themed gym and potential discovery/fossil content later | [Museum galleries](https://www.nmns.edu.tw/en/) |
| Dakeng Hiking Trails / 大坑步道 | Wooded hills and distinctive hiking routes | Forest/hill habitat and a fictional trailhead gym | [Dakeng Hiking Trails](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/attractions/intro/893) |

The first six are the strongest identity anchors in this proposal. The museum and Dakeng round out science and hill/forest exploration. Eight is a useful starting shortlist, not a requirement to use exactly eight gyms or implement all of them in the first demo.

## Strong alternatives or later additions

- **Dajia Jenn Lann Temple / 大甲鎮瀾宮:** a strong candidate if religious heritage and Mazu culture should have a dedicated anchor. [Official visitor information](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/Attractions/Intro/856).
- **Luce Memorial Chapel / 路思義教堂:** a candidate for its distinct architectural silhouette and Tunghai district representation.
- **Miyahara / 宮原眼科:** a candidate for an old-city food/heritage landmark close to the station area.
- **Shen Ji New Village / 審計新村:** a candidate for a creative-shop district near the Greenway cluster.
- **National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts / 國立臺灣美術館:** an alternative cultural anchor if the user prefers art to science or wants both museums represented.

The latter four are listed in the [official Taichung attractions catalog](https://travel.taichung.gov.tw/en/attractions/listbyclassification); detailed visual briefs and factual visitor copy should be checked against their individual primary sources after selection.

## Compression implications to decide after the shortlist

Greenway, Civic Square, CMP, and nearby cultural stops can share a compact district instead of each consuming a separate large map. A district can still contain multiple landmark entrances if those landmarks are later selected as distinct gyms.

Reserve some of the compressed world for coast and hills. Gaomei and Dakeng provide different environments from urban parks without requiring the original real-world travel distances. Final route directions, exact adjacency, map size, and gym order are not decided in this document.
